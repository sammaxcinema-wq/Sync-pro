SyncMaster Pro Architecture & DSP Specifications

High-precision CEP Multicamera Audio Synchronization Engine for Adobe Premiere Pro CC.

Sample-Accurate Precision

Performs audio alignment at sub-frame granularity (1/48,000th of a second), bypassing Premiere's 1-frame boundary limits using audio time units.

GCC-PHAT DSP

Generalized Cross-Correlation with Phase Transform weighting ensures noise-robust synchronization even with reverberant camera scratch mics.

Node.js & JSX CEP Bridge

Dual-layer execution bridge combining HTML5/Node.js high-speed computation with Adobe ExtendScript CSInterface for non-blocking timeline edits.

    • macOS: /Library/Application Support/Adobe/CEP/extensions/SyncMasterPro

    • Windows Registry: Set PlayerDebugMode to 1 under HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\Adobe\CSXS.11.
    • macOS Terminal: Execute defaults write com.adobe.CSXS.11 PlayerDebugMode 1.
  4. Launch Extension: Open Premiere Pro, navigate to Window > Extensions > SyncMaster Pro.
